About Kottakkal Furniture Palace

Located at Thottumughom, Aluva (near Mahilalayam Bridge, opposite Al-Saj Kitchen), we have been crafting premium wooden furniture for over 25 years. Our showroom offers solid teak, rosewood and engineered wood pieces – from sofas and beds to custom wardrobes and dining sets.

We offer free delivery across Kochi & Aluva, 0% EMI options, and personalized design services. Every piece is built to last, blending traditional Kerala craftsmanship with modern aesthetics.
